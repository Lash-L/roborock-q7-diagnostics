Required Setup: How to Put Vacuum into Diagnostic Mode (Activates Port 6001)
[Hide Steps]
PASS 1 • UNCOVER CLIFF SENSORS
1. Raise robot off floor on a box/stand so all cliff sensors are NOT covered and see open air.
2. Hold both Left & Right sides of front bumper inward.
3. Hold Home + Power together. Keep holding until the power-up music is done.
4. The LEDs will turn a whitish-red color to confirm gate clearance.
5. Turn robot fully off and wait 15 seconds.
PASS 2 • FACTORY AP BOOT
1. Place vacuum on a clear, level floor with front bumper released.
2. Hold Home + Power together. Keep holding until the power-up music is done.
3. The LEDs will turn a whitish-red color in this mode as well.
4. Robot will start broadcasting its factory Wi-Fi network.
PASS 3 • CONNECT WI-FI
1. On your PC, connect to the Wi-Fi matching the robot's Serial Number (or DEFAULTSN).
2. Password: test3irobotix.
3. Robot IP is 192.168.5.1 (ignore "no internet" warning).
PASS 4 • RUN DIAGNOSTICS
1. Click Start Diagnostics (Connect) below.
2. The tool streams live 36-tag Nanopb telemetry: optical cliffs, motors, IMU, ultrasonic carpet, SLAM, and bumpers.

⚠️ MCU Hardware Drop Interlock: When the vacuum is elevated or upside-down, the suspension springs extend the drive wheels down (left_wheel_up open circuit) and cliff sensors see open air (0 ADC). The STM32 MCU automatically locks out wheel motors and high-speed fans for finger safety. To test drive wheels or blower fan, place the robot flat on the floor or hold the wheel suspension compressed into the chassis.
